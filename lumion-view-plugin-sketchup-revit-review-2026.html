Lumion View is now Axogram. Here is the Revit and SketchUp setup.

Lumion View was discontinued on 15 September 2026 and folded into Axogram, a free-to-start plugin for SketchUp, Revit and Archicad on Windows. Revit users who want a live link to Lumion Pro need a different plugin, LiveSync. This guide covers both, with prices as listed on 28 October 2026. ArchiGen has not tested either.

Is there a Lumion plugin for Revit?

Two. Axogram, which replaced Lumion View in September 2026, renders inside Revit 2025 or newer on Windows and is free to start. Lumion LiveSync for Revit, also free, links Revit 2021 to 2027 with Lumion Pro, which costs $1,149 a year as listed on 28 October 2026.

The May version of this page reviewed Lumion View on "three live SD jobs," with setup times, a material-remapping percentage and a star rating. ArchiGen ran no such jobs, and those details are gone. So, it turns out, is Lumion View. What follows is built from Lumion's support pages, its plans page and the Autodesk App Store, plus Chaos's and D5's own pricing, all opened on 28 October 2026.

What happened to Lumion View?

It had a short life. Lumion opened View to Lumion Pro subscribers as an Early Access SketchUp plugin in spring 2025, describing it in its launch post as "a real-time design companion that works inside SketchUp." CG Channel reported the first public release as Windows-only for SketchUp 2025, rental only, at an MSRP of $229 a year. A Mac version for SketchUp followed in September 2025, and View for Revit arrived through the Autodesk App Store with ray-traced display, section-box diagrams and "instant 4K renders." An archived copy of Lumion's plans page from 19 May 2026 lists View at $229 billed annually for SketchUp, Revit and Archicad. The old article's "about $24 a month" was wrong even then.

Then, on 15 September 2026, Lumion's support page "From Lumion View to Axogram" said it plainly: "Lumion View has been discontinued as a separate product and evolved into Axogram." Downloads were removed, and View "is no longer available for new purchases." Existing View users get paid Axogram access, according to the same page. The old product URL now redirects to Axogram's page.

What does Axogram do, and what does it cost?

Lumion now pitches the plugin at diagrams as much as renders. The product page lists eight styles, "Blueprint, Clay, Dual Tone, Ink, Polar, Realistic, Soft Vibe, and Wood," with real-time sun, shadow, background and atmosphere controls. The Axogram FAQ fills in the rest: it is "a plugin, not a standalone program," supports "SketchUp 2024 or newer," "Revit 2025 or newer" and "Archicad 25 or newer," and the released plugins "require Windows"; SketchUp for macOS is "in development" with no date. It renders JPG and PNG stills, PNG with a transparent background. The FAQ also says it "does not currently provide a separate Lumion-style material library."

How do you set up the plugin in SketchUp, Revit and Archicad?

Lumion's download and access guide gives three routes, all Windows:

  1. SketchUp. Open the Extension Warehouse from the menu or toolbar, search for "Lumion" or "Axogram," and install. A browser download can be installed manually instead.
  2. Revit. Download from the guide or the Autodesk Marketplace, close Revit, run the .EXE or .MSI and follow the setup wizard. If it does not appear, the FAQ says to close Revit, restart the computer and check the plugin was installed with the required Windows permissions.
  3. Archicad. Close Archicad, then run the downloaded .EXE from the guide or your Lumion Account.

On first launch, click the toolbar icon, accept the terms, and sign in with a Lumion Account. "All users get an Axogram license (free version) automatically added to their account when logging in." One gap to check before you install: View for Revit ran on older Revit releases, while Axogram's floor is Revit 2025. A practice on Revit 2023 or 2024 loses the in-Revit viewport.

What does Lumion LiveSync for Revit do instead?

It links rather than renders. The Autodesk App Store listing says "Lumion LiveSync lets you sync your Revit model directly with Lumion Pro," free to install, with real-time model and camera sync and a Collada exporter. Its help page sets compatibility at "Revit 2021 - 2027 and Lumion 8.5 and newer." To use it: install from the App Store, open the Add-ins tab, click LiveSync, open a project in Lumion, switch Revit to a 3D view and press Play. The changelog notes that LiveSync is now a lightweight separate plugin and "all Lumion View functionality is moved to the plugin Axogram."

LiveSync only makes sense with Lumion Pro, which Lumion lists at $1,149 a year billed upfront, or $1,499 for a floating seat, as listed on 28 October 2026. The old article's "about $125 a month" for Pro was the floating-seat price. Pro has a 14-day free trial and "is not officially supported on Mac computers." Lumion is published by Act-3D B.V., per the App Store listing.

Who should install which?

If you want quick diagrams and stills without leaving SketchUp, Revit 2025 or later, or Archicad on Windows, Axogram's free plan costs nothing to try and the watermark tells you when you have outgrown it. If your Revit model needs to become a Lumion Pro scene with animation and a full asset library, LiveSync is the plugin and Pro is the bill. If you are on a Mac, or on Revit 2023 or 2024, Lumion's in-modeller option is not for you today. Our budget tiers guide places these against AI renderers.

Lumion renamed the product before most people finished reviewing it. Check the name on the download.
